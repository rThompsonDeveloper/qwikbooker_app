QwikBooker - Full Authentication & Role-Based Workflow

🌐 Public Access Behavior

Route: /

Behavior:

If the user is not authenticated, check for the presence of a /[business-name] segment:

❌ If no business-name is present: show message:

"To access your booking portal, please use your unique business link. Example: app.qwikbooker.com/lawncare"

✅ If a business-name is present:

Show the login form

Dynamically check if the business has multiple store locations

✅ If multiple: show a store selection dropdown in the login form

❌ If single store: do not show store selection

🔐 Authentication Flow

Login Behavior:

On submit, send login credentials + selected store (if applicable)

If login fails: show validation or credential error

If login succeeds:

Determine user role:

✅ Business Admin: redirect to /dashboard

✅ Store Staff: redirect to /[business-name]/[store-name]/dashboard

🧠 Authenticated Behavior

All users:

JWT or session must validate before showing protected routes

If token/session invalid: redirect to /[business-name] login

👤 Role Permissions Breakdown

🧑‍💼 Business Admin (Full Access)

Can edit:

Business name

Logo and profile info

Company-wide scheduling rules

Can manage:

All stores (create, delete, update)

All staff (create, assign to store, change roles)

Payment setup + integration (Stripe)

Company-wide analytics

Their own store's calendar, bookings, and availability (if a solo operator or first-time setup)

✅ If they are a solo provider (e.g., hair stylist or independent contractor):

They do not need to create themselves as staff

Their business account automatically has access to calendar management, store setup, and booking integrations as both the admin and operator

Can configure and preview their calendar UI in real-time:

Left side of screen shows scheduling configuration (availability, booking rules, padding, etc.)

Right side renders a live calendar preview as updates are made

Ensures a WYSIWYG (What You See Is What You Get) experience before integration

Can sync with Google Calendar:

One-click OAuth flow to connect their Google account

View and push updates to and from their Google Calendar in real-time

Allows them to see QwikBooker events on their phone and vice versa

🧑‍🏫 Store Manager

Only has access to their assigned store

Can:

Manage staff within the store

View full store analytics

Edit store schedules

View and manage their own calendar and bookings

🧑‍🔧 Staff (Basic Access)

Can:

See only their schedule

View their upcoming appointments

Access their own clients only

❌ Cannot:

See other staff

View store analytics

Manage users or payment systems

📨 Staff Creation & Onboarding

Staff can be created by either Admins or Store Managers

On creation:

Collect info: email, phone number, role, and store assignment

Trigger email invite with:

Temporary login credentials

Direct link to their business's login page

After first login, staff can:

Change their email/password

Complete their profile

🔗 Integration & Customer Access

Integration Section (in Admin/Manager Dashboard):

Show unique public links to booking pages or calendars

Allow users to:

Share booking pages to social media

Embed the calendar into external websites (planned feature)

🚦 Edge Cases

If store staff tries to access a different store or business URL → deny access

If someone tries to log in with no business context in the URL → show redirect prompt

If user is already authenticated and visits / → redirect to their proper dashboard based on role
